# Device pictures

The ATK A9 Air Master+ and N9 V2 Ultimate assets are original schematic device icons drawn for identification; they are not product photographs or vendor artwork.

Exported from tracked assets in [Peripheral Desk](https://github.com/chenran0408/peripheral-desk) at commit `728f28d5c69e735590c4754fd40fbbfd57b8f865` using `git show`, without reading or replacing working-tree pictures. Device images are included for identification. Product names and artwork belong to their respective owners; this project is independent of those brands.

The G305 preview uses an original neutral mouse illustration created for this project, not a vendor product photograph.

The G304 preview reuses that original neutral mouse illustration for identification; it is not a vendor product photograph.
