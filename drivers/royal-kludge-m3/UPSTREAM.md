- Source: [shanuflash/nibble](https://github.com/shanuflash/nibble), `src/Devices/RoyalKludge/M3Mouse.cs` and `RkLink.cs`. MIT, copyright 2026 shanuflash. Protocol facts were independently implemented; no source code copied.
